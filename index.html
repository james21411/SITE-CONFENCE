About

The 1st Conference on Advances in Computer Science and Educational Technologies (EdTech) for Sustainable Development (CAITED) aims to bring together researchers, educators, technologists, and policymakers to explore the intersection of computer science, EdTech, and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

It serves as a platform for interdisciplinary dialogue between computer scientists, education specialists, policymakers, decision-makers, development practitioners, and Tech 4 Society experts.

Call For Paper

The CAITED organizing committee invites you to submit your article for potential presentation at the 2026 edition of this conference in one of the following thematic areas (non-exhaustive list):

Thematic areas (TRACKs)

  • Contribution of digital technology to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
  • Computer science and environmental transition
  • Digitization and reduction of environmental footprint
  • Smart technologies for sustainable natural resource management
  • Environmental challenges and digital sobriety in Africa

  • E-learning, hybrid teaching, and distance learning
  • Artificial intelligence applied to education
  • Open Educational Resources (OER)
  • Training and support for teachers in educational technology
  • Educational technologies adapted to local African realities

  • Réduction de la fracture numérique touchant les jeunes et les femmes
  • Accès équitable des jeunes filles et des femmes aux TIC et à l'éducation numérique
  • Numérique et éducation inclusive pour les jeunes vulnérables
  • Leadership féminin et participation des jeunes dans le secteur numérique
  • Technologies numériques comme leviers d'autonomisation sociale et économique

  • Intelligence artificielle et apprentissage automatique
  • Internet des objets (IoT) et environnements intelligents
  • Big Data et analyse des données pour l'aide à la décision
  • Blockchain et technologies de confiance numérique
  • Réalité virtuelle, réalité augmentée et environnements immersifs éducatifs

  • Evolution of educational paradigms from Education 1.0 to Education 5.0
  • Contributions and limitations of artificial intelligence in educational systems
  • Adapting teaching practices to Generations Z and Alpha requirements
  • Adaptive learning, personalized learning, and intelligent support
  • The role of teachers, ethics, and humanization of education in the age of AI

  • Privacy and personnal data protection
  • Cybersecurity of educational and institutional systems
  • AI ethics, algorithmic bias, and social responsibility
  • Public policies and regulatory frameworks for digital technology
  • Promoting responsible digital citizenship

  • Technology entrepreneurship driven by young people and women
  • Digital innovation and youth employability
  • Strengthening women's digital and entrepreneurial skills
  • University–business–incubator partnerships
  • International cooperation to support youth and women's digital initiatives
